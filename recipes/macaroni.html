Macaroni and Cheese

Description

The ultimate American comfort food! Tender macaroni coated in a rich, creamy cheese sauce with a golden crumb topping. Always satisfying and irresistibly indulgent.

Ingredients

  • 1 (16 ounce) package elbow macaroni
  • 6 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 6 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons McCormick® Mustard, Ground
  • 1 ½ teaspoons McCormick® Black Pepper, Coarse Grind
  • ¼ teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon McCormick® Garlic Powder
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 4 cups milk
  • 6 cups shredded sharp Cheddar cheese
  • 1 ½ cups panko bread crumbs
  • 1 teaspoon McCormick® Paprika

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 400 degrees F (204 degrees C). Cook macaroni in a large saucepan according to package directions for al dente pasta. Rinse under cold water. Drain well.
  2. Melt butter in the same saucepan on medium heat. Sprinkle with flour, ground mustard, black pepper, garlic powder, and salt. Cook, stirring until well blended, about 2 minutes. Gradually stir in milk. Continue cooking, stirring constantly until sauce begins to thickens, about 3 minutes.
  3. Add cheese, stirring until melted and smooth. Remove from heat. Add macaroni. Toss gently to coat. Transfer to a greased 9x13-inch baking dish. Mix panko and paprika. Sprinkle evenly over top.
  4. Bake until bubbly and golden brown on top, 25 to 30 minutes. Let stand 5 minutes before serving.
